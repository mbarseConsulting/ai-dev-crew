## Changed — rule becomes wrong or incomplete

**`references/bp-ws.md`, transport rule.** SSE under HTTP/1.1 is capped by the browser's 6 connections per origin; the rule recommends SSE without saying it needs HTTP/2. Source: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Server-sent_events/Using_server-sent_events.

**`references/bp-api-rest.md`, idempotency rule.** The header has a de-facto name, `Idempotency-Key` (draft-ietf-httpapi-idempotency-key-header, expired 2025-10-15, not an RFC); the rule leaves it unnamed. Source: https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-httpapi-idempotency-key-header/.

**`references/bp-kafka.md`, key and ordering rule.** Under a share group (KIP-932, Spring Kafka 4.1 / Kafka 4.2) records fan out record by record across members of the same partition, so a deliberate key gives no ordering and only at-least-once applies. Source: https://docs.spring.io/spring-kafka/reference/kafka/kafka-queues.html.

## Changed — rule missing

**`technos/spring.md`.** `@MockBean` and `@SpyBean` are deprecated in Boot 3.4 and removed in 4.0, replaced by `@MockitoBean` and `@MockitoSpyBean`. Source: https://github.com/spring-projects/spring-boot/wiki/Spring-Boot-4.0-Migration-Guide.

**`technos/spring.md`.** Spring Kafka's `DefaultErrorHandler` retries 9 times then logs and drops the record unless a `DeadLetterPublishingRecoverer` is wired explicitly, and the dead-letter topic is not created automatically. Source: https://docs.spring.io/spring-kafka/reference/kafka/annotation-error-handling.html.

**`references/bp-ws.md`.** No rule validates the `Origin` header at the handshake; RFC 6455 §4.2.2 leaves it to the server, and a server that skips it accepts a socket from any page with the visitor's cookies. Source: https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6455.html.

**`references/bp-ws.md`.** No rule on `Sec-WebSocket-Protocol` negotiation: the server picks zero or one of what the client offered, and a conformant client aborts on anything else. Source: https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6455.html, https://docs.spring.io/spring-framework/reference/web/websocket.html.

**`references/bp-ws.md`.** WebSocket over HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 (Extended CONNECT, RFC 8441 and 9220) rides the multiplexed connection and survives proxies and per-host caps a raw Upgrade does not. Stack-dependent: flag, don't fix. Sources: https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8441.html, https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9220.html.

**`references/bp-api-rest.md`.** RFC 9457 §4.2 creates an IANA "HTTP Problem Types" registry; nothing tells authors to check it before minting a `type` URI. Source: https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9457.html.

**`references/bp-api-rest.md`.** `RateLimit` and `RateLimit-Policy` response headers (draft-ietf-httpapi-ratelimit-headers, active, not an RFC). Flag, don't fix while it is a draft. Source: https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-httpapi-ratelimit-headers/.
